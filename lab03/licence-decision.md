**Decision:** MIT License

**Rationale:** We selected the MIT License because it offers maximum permissiveness, exceptional simplicity, and seamless compatibility with our existing dependencies, notably GoogleTest (BSD-3-Clause). As an academic project developed at ITC, Club Hub is intended to serve as a reusable, extensible foundation for subsequent cohorts of student developers and campus clubs. The short, straightforward text of the MIT License eliminates legal complexity for future contributors while allowing potential integration into broader university systems or external applications without restrictive copyleft encumbrances. The teaching assistant confirmed there is no departmental requirement for copyleft licensing, making MIT the ideal fit for our educational and collaborative goals.
