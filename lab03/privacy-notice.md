# Privacy Notice · ITC Club Hub
Version 1.1 · 2026-11-21

## Who we are
We are the ITC Club Hub team at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia. We help you discover campus clubs and register for events.

## What we collect and why
When you register, we collect:
- **studentId** to confirm your enrollment and stop duplicate accounts.
- **name** to greet you and check you in at events.
- **email** to send your registration confirmation and event tickets.
- **clubMemberships** to show which clubs you join.
- **attendance** to help clubs manage room capacity.

Optionally, you can enable **emailReminders**, share your **phone** for urgent SMS notices, or share **faculty and year** in surveys for campus statistics.

## Who sees it
Only you, event organisers, club leaders, and administrators see your data. Organisers only see a masked studentId and cannot see your phone in clear text. We never sell or share your data externally.

## How long
We keep your profile while you study at ITC. When you delete your account or graduate, we erase your phone number and reminder settings immediately. We retain your studentId and name for 1 year after a deletion request to prevent duplicate accounts, then delete them permanently. We aggregate attendance and surveys into club totals each semester and purge individual records.

## Your choices
Optional features start turned off. You can use Club Hub without giving a phone number or survey answers. You can change your reminder settings or withdraw consent anytime in account settings. You can also request a copy of your data or delete your account.

## Contact
Questions or deletion requests? Email us at clubhub@itc.edu.kh or visit our team in the student center.
