# Privacy Notice · ITC Club Hub
Version 1.0 · 2026-11-11

## Who we are
We are the ITC Club Hub team, a student-led project at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia. We help you discover campus clubs and register for club events.

## What we collect and why
When you register, we collect:
- Your **studentId** to confirm your enrollment and stop duplicate registrations.
- Your **name** to greet you and check you in at events.
- Your **email** to send your account confirmation and event tickets.
- Your **clubMemberships** to show which clubs you belong to.
- Your **attendance** to help clubs plan room capacity.

Optionally, you can give us your **phone** number if you want text reminders for urgent event changes.

## Who sees it
Only you, event organisers, club leaders, and system administrators see your information. Organisers only see a masked version of your studentId and cannot see your phone number in clear text. We never sell or share your data with external companies.

## How long
We keep your account while you study at ITC or until you delete it. We erase event attendance records at the end of each semester after turning them into club totals. If you give us your phone number, we erase it immediately when you withdraw consent.

## Your choices
Optional features start turned off. You can use Club Hub without giving a phone number. You can change your reminder settings or withdraw consent anytime in your account settings. You can also request a copy of your data or delete your account.

## Contact
Questions or deletion requests? Email us at clubhub@itc.edu.kh or visit our team in the student center.
