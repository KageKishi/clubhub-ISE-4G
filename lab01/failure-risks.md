# Failure risks · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-**-**)

| #  |                     How the project could fail               | Chapter 01 topic | Early warning sign | First countermeasure | Owner |
|----|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|--------------------|----------------------|-------|
| F1 | Inaccurate or failed attendance logging due to session/login timeouts and authentication bugs during peak event check-in times. | Problem Statement & Scope | High rate of user complaints about login failures and manual attendance override requests after club meetings. | Implement robust session management, local error caching, and fallback offline check-in options. | Lead Developer |
| F2 | Low adoption rate among students and club leaders who continue using legacy group chats and paper sheets. | Product Vision & Workarounds | Fewer than 20% of active campus clubs creating accounts or uploading course/event materials within the first month. | Conduct hands-on onboarding sessions with club leaders and introduce single-tap QR check-in to prove convenience over paper. | Project Manager |
| F3 | Scope creep caused by attempting to build full backend features in C++ while designing complex web/mobile UIs within a single semester. | Scope Definition & Planning | Team missing early milestones or spending disproportionate time debugging C++ server bindings instead of core logic. | Strictly enforce the defined Scope boundaries (C++ core backend/session logic only, web/mobile frontend UI modelled only). | Technical Lead |
| F4 | Resistance or lack of support from lecturers/advisors due to privacy concerns or misalignment with university policies. | Stakeholder Management | Slow response times from faculty advisors when requested to review or pilot the platform. | Schedule early administrative briefing demos highlighting automated reporting and data security controls. | Product Owner |
